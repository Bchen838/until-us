Until Us needs an iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Until Us. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then tap the widget area. You can design your widgets in the app's Widgets tab.

How do I link with my partner?

In the Us tab, tap Invite (it shows your partner's name) and send the link to your partner. When they open it on their iPhone, you share one countdown. You both need to be signed in to iCloud on your iPhones.

My partner's changes aren't showing up

  • Open Until Us on both phones while they're online. Changes usually arrive within a minute.
  • Check that both of you are signed in to iCloud in the Settings app.
  • If iCloud storage is full, the app tells you whose iCloud is full. Your shared countdown is stored in the iCloud of the person who sent the invite, so that person may need to free up space.

The distance isn't showing

Both of you need to allow location for Until Us in the Settings app. Your partner only ever sees the distance and your city, never your exact location.

What is Until Us Plus?

A one-time purchase that unlocks Plus for you and your linked partner: every upcoming visit, unlimited highlights, the Framed style, a new widget photo every day, the Getting Closer and Our Visits widgets, and every phrase style. It isn't a subscription.

Restore a purchase: in the Us tab, open Settings, then Until Us Plus, and tap Restore Purchases.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
