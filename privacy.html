Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Until Us has no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you add lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account, and is shared only with the partner you link with. The developer can't see any of it.

Who we are

Until Us is an iPhone app for long-distance couples, made by Brian Chen, an independent developer. "We" in this policy means the developer of Until Us.

What the app stores

  • You and your partner: the names and profile photos you add.
  • Visits: dates, places and arrival times.
  • Things you make together: goodnights (the time each of you said it), items on your list, goodbye letters (text, and an optional photo and voice recording), visit highlights (photos and short video clips), lines you write for your partner's widget, and your widget designs.
  • Approximate location, only if you allow it: your position rounded to about 1 km, and the name of your city. It's used to show how far apart you are and to notice when you're together.
  • Purchase status: whether you own Until Us Plus.

Where it's stored

Everything is stored on your iPhone. When you link with your partner, it syncs between your two phones through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). Your shared countdown is kept in the iCloud account of the person who sent the invite and counts toward that person's iCloud storage.

Apple runs iCloud under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't run any servers of our own and have no access to your iCloud data.

Who can see it

Only you and the partner you link with. Some things stay on your phone and are never shared: letters you haven't sealed yet, your own custom phrases, your daily photo album, and which letters you've opened. When you seal a goodbye letter it's shared with your partner, and their app keeps it closed until the visit ends.

Your partner sees the distance between you and the name of your city, never your exact location.

Permissions

  • Location: to show the distance between you. You can allow it only while using the app, or also in the background so the distance updates after big moves. To look up your city name, the app sends your rounded location to Apple's map service.
  • Photos: the app uses the system photo picker, so it only sees the photos and videos you choose.
  • Microphone: used only while you record a voice note.
  • Notifications: reminders and partner updates are created on your iPhone. No notification content is sent to us.

You can change any of these at any time in the Settings app.

Purchases

Until Us Plus is sold through the App Store, and Apple processes the payment. We never receive your payment details. The app asks Apple whether you own Plus, and shares only that yes-or-no answer with your linked partner so Plus unlocks for both of you.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up or account with us. Linking uses your iCloud account.
  • No analytics, advertising or third-party tracking code.
  • No selling or sharing of your information with anyone.

Deleting your information

  • Deleting a visit also deletes its list, letters and highlights, for both of you.
  • You can remove a highlight for both of you from the visit's highlights.
  • To stop sharing, open the Us tab, then Settings, then Stop sharing or Leave.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. To remove the copy in your iCloud, open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Storage (on some versions, Manage Account Storage). Choose Until Us there, if it's listed, and delete its data.

Children

Until Us isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
